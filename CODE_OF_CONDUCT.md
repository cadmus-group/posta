# Code of Conduct

We want Posta to be a constructive, respectful place for contributors and users.

## Expectations

Be welcoming to people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and discuss ideas without personal attacks. Be considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and any project-related community space.

Harassment, discriminatory or degrading language, threats, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publication of others' private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close conversations, and restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and applied consistently.

If you experience or witness misconduct, contact a project maintainer privately through a contact method they make available on their GitHub profile. Do not post private or identifying details in a public issue. Maintainers involved in a report should not handle its resolution alone. Reports will be considered discreetly to the extent reasonably possible.

This policy applies to the repository, related project discussions, and spaces where someone officially represents the project.
